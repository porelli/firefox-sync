What the badges and actions mean
  • 2-step: two-step authentication is on.
  • No recovery key: a forgotten password loses the Sync data, unless a device still has it.
  • Inactive: not synced for {{.StaleDays}} days; old devices: not used for as long (ADMIN_STALE_DAYS).
  • Unconfirmed sign-in: a sign-in that never entered its email code. Blocked: by the rate limits, see the account page.
  • Disable signs the account out everywhere and keeps it from signing in again. Its browsers stop syncing within the hour.

Stop the stack, load the dumps into the databases as their own users, start it again. Leave the Sync storage empty: each Firefox uploads its data again, while last night's copy would give back what was changed or deleted since. The steps are in {{if .Kubernetes}}{{else if .Quadlets}}{{else}}{{end}}Backups, Restore{{if $.FxA}}; the accounts need the same {{with .Kubernetes}}Secret and the keys volume {{.FxaKeys}} (or your fxa.keys.existingSecret){{else}}.env and fxa-keys{{end}}{{end}}.
