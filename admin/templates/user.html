About devices and signing out

Inactive: not used for {{.StaleDays}} days (ADMIN_STALE_DAYS), a device that may be gone. Signing out a device ends its session and its tokens: Firefox there asks to sign in again (it can sync for up to an hour with what it has). The account keeps working everywhere else; to lock it out, disable it.

Sign out everywhere ends every session and device of the account; its owner can sign in again right away. Disable also keeps them from signing in. Either way Firefox may sync for up to 1 hour more with the Sync token it has, and the Sync server remembers a checked access token for 5 minutes.
